# Puente Nuevo photographic texture

`puente-nuevo-original.jpg` is the unmodified 2388 × 3528 original photograph:

- Title: Ronda - Puente Nuevo tall
- Photographer: Joe Mabel / Jmabel
- Date: late April or early May 1996
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ronda_-_Puente_Nuevo_tall.jpg
- License: CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Original file SHA-1: 0808e6a5de4e5b558408fbeaaf0f5fc6278670f8

The source pixels are not AI-generated or repainted. `src/puente-nuevo.ts`
corrects the UV mapping by architectural region in the shader. Piers, upper
openings and cornices use different mapping ranges to follow the real stone.
Returns and tunnel surfaces use an unobstructed masonry portion of the same
photograph. The photograph is reused on the reverse facade; it is not a
separate photographic survey of that face. Source shadows and perspective
remain limitations of the single-photo reconstruction.

The adapted photographic texture is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0, with
attribution and the changes identified here and in the site's credits.
`puente_nuevo.webp` is the older cropped derivative of this same source.
